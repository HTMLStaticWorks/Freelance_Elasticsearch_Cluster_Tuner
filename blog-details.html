5 Shard Allocation Tips You Need

By Alex Rivera | May 10, 2026 | 8 min read

Proper shard allocation is the backbone of a healthy Elasticsearch cluster. Without it, you'll face "hot nodes" where one server is doing 90% of the work while the others sit idle.

1. Keep Shards Between 10GB and 50GB

Small shards lead to excessive overhead, while massive shards (over 50GB) make recovery and rebalancing extremely slow. Aim for the sweet spot.

2. Use Node Attributes for Rack Awareness

Ensuring that primary and replica shards aren't on the same physical rack is crucial for high availability. Use node.attr.rack_id to configure this.

In conclusion, monitoring your shard count per node and keeping an eye on the _cluster/allocation/explain API will save you hours of debugging.
